Overview

Understanding what makes people buy smart home products

Narwhal is a smart home appliance company. I was brought on as a user researcher to understand the emotional and contextual factors that drive purchase decisions for high-end smart home products, particularly the Smart Cat Litter Box.

My Role

What I did

  • Conducted "Think-Aloud" user interviews with 10+ target users to identify core emotional drivers
  • Provided cultural and contextual insights for global product strategy alignment
  • Generated 10 research reports with visualized qualitative data
  • Identified friction points that led to 3 core product feature iterations

Reflection

What I learned

This project deepened my appreciation for qualitative research as a design tool. Numbers tell you what's happening; conversations tell you why. The most valuable insights came from the moments users struggled to articulate their feelings.
